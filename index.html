01 / About us

There’s a common thread in all of us.

Common Thread is a student-led nonprofit organization at Miami University. We gather students together to make tie blankets that bring warmth, comfort, and a reminder that someone cares.

Our goal is simple: create a welcoming community while turning a little time and fabric into something meaningful.

02 / What we do
✂

Make blankets

We host hands-on blanket-making events where students can learn, create, and connect.

♡

Give comfort

Our finished blankets can be shared with people and organizations who could use a little extra comfort.

↗

Build community

Every knot is an opportunity to meet someone new and make campus feel a little more connected.
