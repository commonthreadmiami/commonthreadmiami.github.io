01 / About Common Thread

There’s a common thread in all of us.

We believe something as simple as making a blanket can create warmth, connection, and a reminder that someone cares.

Common Thread is a student-led service organization at Miami University. We gather students together for hands-on blanket making while creating a welcoming community on campus.

Our blankets are made to bring comfort to hospice patients at Cleveland Clinic Akron General.
