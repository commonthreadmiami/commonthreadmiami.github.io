01 — Our story

There’s a common thread in all of us.

A blanket can be simple. The feeling behind it doesn't have to be.

Common Thread is a student-led service organization at Miami University. We gather students for hands-on blanket making while creating a welcoming community on campus.

Our handmade tie blankets are created to bring comfort to hospice patients at Cleveland Clinic Akron General.
